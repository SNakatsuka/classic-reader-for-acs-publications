# Privacy Policy

Classic Reader for ACS Publications does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension does not send article text, figures, PDFs, browsing history, account information, or identifiers to any external server.

Settings are stored locally in the browser using Chrome storage.

To display page elements, the extension may request ACS Publications pages that the user is viewing. These requests are made directly from the user’s browser to ACS Publications and are not relayed through a third-party server.
